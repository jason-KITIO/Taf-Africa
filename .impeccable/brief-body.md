# Surface brief — Landing page Taf Africa

## Scope and mode

Persuade. Route `/` (`src/app/page.tsx`), the public landing for a two-sided
freelance marketplace. Mobile-first: the reference screen is a phone.

## Audience, job, action

- **Audience:** both faces at once — clients (PME, particuliers, entrepreneurs)
  and freelances of francophone Africa, mostly on a phone, variable connections.
- **Job:** understand in seconds what Taf Africa is and which side they are on,
  then take the category's action in its working form.
- **Action:** search / browse the market (find a talent, find a mission) and
  reach the two primary CTAs — publish a need (client), create a profile
  (freelance). The candidature is gated (credits/subscription), the browse is free.
- **Proof / content:** the mechanism itself, shown working — a two-sided market
  entry, real-looking mission listings, the gated "postuler" control, Mobile
  Money + Stripe, no company required. **No fabricated testimonials, stats,
  clients, or partner logos** (PRODUCT.md). Every illustrative entry is authored
  at full fidelity and labelled synthetic.

## Constraints

- French UI. Light, trustworthy, restrained — the category's convention.
- Quality bar (brand commitment): Codeur.com + Upwork + Malt craft, Linear polish.
- Chatbot flottant and the FAQ stay. Both client and freelance paths stay visible.
- No invented commercial claims. No "US SaaS" generic look, no cold institutional tone.
- Craft floor bans apply: no eyebrow/kicker above headings, no hero-metric template,
  no equal icon+heading+text card grid as page structure, no gradient text, no
  decorative glass, no hard offset shadows, no emoji/glyph icons.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

**The canon, played straight** — the category standard at its highest finish,
conventions embraced without irony or smuggled quirk. The memorable moment is the
first screen doing the job: a working two-sided market entry where the visitor
picks their side and the offer is already legible and clickable, not a poster
that defers the action below the fold.

## Unresolved decisions

- Real logo asset (monogram only for now), real mission/profile photography,
  real numbers and testimonials — all placeholders until assets exist.
- Legal identity, Mobile Money providers, launch date (PRODUCT.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The market works from the first screen — both sides legible and the
category's action (search / post) already usable, never deferred below the fold.
It refuses the decorative landing that paints a mood and pushes the mechanism
under a scroll.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white paper (#FAFAF7) ground, near-black ink (#14171C),
one amber accent (#E0900E) carrying every primary action and the logo's final
dot; hairline 1px borders, soft offset shadows, 12–16px radii, pill primary
buttons, tabular numerals. Neutral, high-contrast, trust-first — the palette of a
marketplace, not a poster.

STORY: The visitor understands Taf Africa connects clients and freelances across
francophone Africa; believes consulting is free and payment is local (Mobile
Money + card, no company required); and either searches the market or posts a
need / creates a profile.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width sticky nav — wordmark left, links (Talents, Missions,
Tarifs, FAQ), primary "Publier un besoin" button right. Below it, centred: a
one-line hook stating the offer, a two-path segmented entry (Je cherche un talent
/ Je cherche une mission) over a working search field, and directly under it a
live strip of authored mission cards with the gated "Postuler" state visible.
Primary action sits centre, above the fold, at hero scale.

FORM: The category standard (the standing exit), played straight at the craft
bar of Codeur.com, Upwork, Malt and Linear; no ordered-list position — the canon
is the offered convention, not a dealt card. seed:918cd4be8c57c633
